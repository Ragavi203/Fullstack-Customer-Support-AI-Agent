# Troubleshooting guides

Use the guide that matches the caller's plan. Do not walk a Prepaid line through a feature it does not have.

## No service or SOS only
Tiers: all

When the phone shows no service or SOS only, first check the outage map for the caller's area. If there is no reported outage, turn airplane mode on for ten seconds and off again, then restart the phone. If the line is past_due or suspended, service is limited until the balance is paid. If the problem continues on a physical SIM, reseat the SIM card. If it continues after a restart, open an escalation so a network engineer can check the tower.

## Dropped calls and Wi-Fi Calling
Tiers: plus, business

Plus and Business lines include Wi-Fi Calling. When calls drop indoors, turn on Wi-Fi Calling in the phone settings and confirm the emergency address on the account. Dropped calls in a single building are usually coverage, not a fault on the line. If calls drop everywhere, restart the phone and update the carrier settings before escalating.

## Mobile data not working
Tiers: all

When mobile data stops working, confirm mobile data is on and the access point name is linden.data. Prepaid lines slow to 256 kbps after 15 GB of high-speed data in the refill period, which is a plan limit and not a fault. Plus lines get 50 GB of priority data, then may slow at busy cell sites. A carrier settings update fixes most data problems after a network outage.

## eSIM activation and transfer
Tiers: plus, business

Plus and Business lines can use an eSIM. To move a line to a new phone, start the transfer from the old phone, or scan the one-time activation QR code sent to the billing email. Each QR code works once. Linden Care can resend the code, but a SIM swap to an unknown device always needs a human teammate. Prepaid lines use a physical SIM only.

## Outage checks
Tiers: all

If the caller reports an outage, check whether one phone is affected or the whole area. Area outages are posted on the outage map with an estimated restore time. A single phone with no service is handled with the no service guide and is not announced as a network outage. Outage credits follow the caller's service level.
