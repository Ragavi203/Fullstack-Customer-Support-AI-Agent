# Billing and payments

Billing changes go through update_account or the billing tool. Do not invent invoice numbers.

## Plan prices and monthly charges
Tiers: all

What each plan costs: Prepaid is $30 per line for each 30-day refill. Plus is $65 per line each month. Business is $45 per line each month. The monthly price is the per-line price times the number of lines on the account. Taxes and fees are included in the charge amount the billing tool returns. The last charge on the account is the only charge Linden Care can discuss for a refund.

## Billing email
Tiers: all

The account holder can change the billing email when the contract is active, past_due, or suspended, so the bill reaches the right person. Cancelled accounts are read-only. The new value must be an email address. Linden Care confirms the previous and the new billing email after update_account succeeds.

## Late payments and suspension
Tiers: plus, business

A failed autopay payment sets the contract to past_due and records the amount owed. After 30 days past due the lines are suspended and can only call emergency services. Linden Care cannot take a payment in chat. Tell the caller the amount due and that refunds stay blocked until it is paid.
