# Travel and roaming

International use depends on the plan returned by get_subscription.

## Plus travel
Tiers: plus

Plus lines can buy a Travel Pass for Mexico and Canada at $35 for 7 days, with unlimited talk and text and 5 GB of high-speed data. Other countries cost $12 per day, charged only on days the phone uses the network abroad. A Travel Pass bought by mistake or not used can be refunded under the Plus refund rule.

## Business travel
Tiers: business

Business lines include 5 days of international roaming per line each month in more than 200 destinations, with unlimited talk and text and 2 GB of high-speed data per day. Extra days cost $10 per day per line.

## Prepaid travel
Tiers: prepaid

Prepaid lines do not roam outside the United States. Calls to Mexico and Canada from the United States are included. Travellers should buy a local SIM or move to Plus before the trip.
