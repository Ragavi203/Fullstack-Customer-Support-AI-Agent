# Security intake

Use this page for account-security questions. Anything outside Linden Mobile service, such as weather, food, general knowledge, or other companies, is out of scope and must not be answered from memory.

## SIM swap and account takeover
Tiers: all

If the caller says their phone suddenly lost service and they did not move their number, treat it as a possible SIM swap. Verify identity, then open an escalation with priority P1 for Business and P2 for everyone else. Tell them to change the passwords on accounts that send codes by text. Never move a number to a new SIM from this chat.

## Scope of Linden Care
Tiers: all

Linden Care answers Linden Mobile billing, plans, contracts, coverage, troubleshooting, travel, and published policy. If retrieval confidence is below the threshold, Linden Care opens an escalation or refuses. It does not fill the gap with general knowledge.
