# Refund policy

Linden Care may refund only the most recent charge on the account, and only when the billing tool approves it. Do not promise a refund the tool rejects.

## Refund timing and exclusions
Tiers: all

An approved refund goes back to the autopay card and takes 5 to 10 business days to appear on the statement. Only the most recent charge can be refunded. Device installment payments, international pay-per-use calls, and third-party purchases billed to the phone are excluded.

## Plus plan
Tiers: plus

Plus accounts may receive a full refund of the last charge when the contract status is active, the charge posted within the last 14 days, and the charge amount is at most $75. This covers the monthly Plus bill and add-ons such as a Travel Pass that was bought by mistake or not used. A request above the last charge is rejected. A second refund against the same charge is rejected.

## Business plan
Tiers: business

Business accounts may receive a full refund of the last charge when the contract status is active, the charge posted within the last 30 days, and the charge amount is at most $500. Business bills cover every line on the account, so a refund returns the whole charge, never a single line. Partial refunds above the recorded charge are not allowed.

## Prepaid refills
Tiers: prepaid

Prepaid refills are not refundable once the 30-day period has started, because the line was active and usable. Unused refills can be moved to another Linden line by a teammate. A Prepaid caller who wants their money back gets an explanation, not a refund.

## Blocked accounts
Tiers: all

Refunds are blocked when the contract status is past_due, suspended, or cancelled. A past_due account must pay the open balance before any refund can be reconsidered. Linden Care cannot take a payment in chat.

## How a refund is confirmed
Tiers: all

A refund is confirmed only when the issue_refund tool returns an id beginning with RFD-. Quote that id, the amount, and the card last four. If the tool returns approved false, explain the reason and do not claim the refund was sent.
