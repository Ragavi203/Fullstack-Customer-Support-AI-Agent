# Service levels (SLA)

Support response targets and outage credits depend on the plan returned by get_subscription. Do not quote another plan's target.

## Business SLA
Tiers: business

The Business SLA includes a dedicated support line answered around the clock. Severity P1 (every line at a site has no service) gets a first response within 15 minutes, all day. P2 gets a first response within 1 hour. A confirmed network outage longer than 4 hours earns a credit of one full day of service for every affected line.

## Plus SLA
Tiers: plus

The Plus SLA gives a first response within 4 hours for P1 and by the next day for P2. A confirmed network outage longer than 24 hours earns a credit of one full day of service on the bill.

## Prepaid SLA
Tiers: prepaid

The Prepaid SLA is chat and help-center support with no guaranteed response time and no outage credit. Prepaid lines get one extra day added to the refill period when a confirmed outage lasts longer than 48 hours.

## Severity definitions
Tiers: all

P1 means a line or site has no service at all, or emergency calling is affected. P2 means degraded service with a workaround, such as slow data or dropped calls in one area. P3 means a question, a how-to, or a billing clarification. Priority on an escalation ticket follows the plan: Business P1, Plus P2, Prepaid P3, unless the caller already reported a complete loss of service.
